No Server. No Tracking. No Ads.

Your financial data is stored exclusively on your device. We do not collect, sell, or share any of your personal or financial information with third parties.

Introduction

Savora is a personal finance management app designed to help you track income, expenses, and savings effortlessly. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our Service.

By using Savora, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please discontinue use of the app.

Information Collection & Use

Your financial data — transactions, categories, balances, notes — is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted anywhere.

Your account email address is synced with Firebase (Google) so we can identify your account and, in the future, support features like backup and sign-in across devices. Your financial data itself is not part of this sync — only your email address is stored on Firebase's servers.

Data Storage

All app data is stored in a local SQLite database on your device. This includes:

  • Transaction records (amount, category, date, notes)
  • Custom categories you create
  • App settings and preferences
  • Profile information (name, email)

Your email address is additionally stored with Firebase, as described above. Uninstalling the app will permanently delete all locally stored data from your device.

Log Data

In the event of an app error or crash, basic diagnostic information may be collected automatically by the underlying platform (iOS / Android). This may include your device model, OS version, app version, and the time of the error.

This data is used solely to improve app stability and does not include any of your financial data.

Third-Party Services

Savora uses the following third-party / open-source services:

  • Firebase (Google) — stores your account email address only, for identifying your account
  • Expo — app framework, no personal data collected
  • React Native — UI framework, no personal data collected
  • SQLite — local database, data stays on device

If future updates introduce optional cloud backup, this policy will be updated and you will be notified in advance.

Data Export

Savora allows you to export your transaction data as a PDF or JSON file directly from the app. Exported files are shared via your device's native share sheet and are your responsibility once exported.

We have no access to any data you choose to export or share.

Children's Privacy

Savora is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action promptly.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data within the app. Since your financial data is stored locally on your device, its security largely depends on your own device's security settings (screen lock, biometrics, etc.). Your email address stored with Firebase is protected using Google's standard security infrastructure.

We recommend keeping your device updated and secured with a strong passcode or biometric authentication.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected within the app with an updated effective date at the top of this page.

Continued use of Savora after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
